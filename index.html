A4 Size Modern Photo Frame with Glass Perfect for Home & Office Decort

  • A4 Size (8.3 x 11.7 inches) – Ideal for photos, documents & art prints
  • Modern & Elegant Look – Black border design fits any decor
  • Durable Front Glass (2-3mm thick) – Protects your photo from dust, scratches & moisture
  • Perfect for Wall Mounting or Tabletop Display – Vertical or horizontal orientation

Whether you’re decorating your home, office, or gifting someone special, this high-quality photo frame suits every purpose with its minimalist and modern Indian design.
